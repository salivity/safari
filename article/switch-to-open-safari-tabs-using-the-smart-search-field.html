Switch to Open Safari Tabs Using the Smart Search Field

Managing dozens of open browser tabs can quickly become overwhelming, often leading to duplicate tabs of the exact same webpage. Fortunately, Apple's Safari web browser includes a built-in feature within its Smart Search Field that allows you to jump directly to an already open tab when you start typing its matching URL or page title. This article provides a straightforward guide on how to use the "Switch to Tab" feature in Safari on both Mac and iOS devices to keep your browsing organized and efficient.

How to Switch to an Open Tab on a Mac

When using Safari on macOS, the Smart Search Field acts as both an address bar and a tab search tool. Follow these steps to jump to an active tab:

  1. Select the Smart Search Field: Click the address bar at the top of the Safari window, or press the keyboard shortcut Command + L to highlight it instantly.
  2. Type the URL or Page Title: Begin typing the web address or the title of the page you know is already open in another tab.
  3. Locate the "Switch to Tab" Option: As you type, a dropdown menu of suggestions will appear. Look for the matching webpage that features the words Switch to Tab on the right side of the suggestion box.
  4. Select the Tab: Click on the suggestion with your mouse, or use the Down Arrow key on your keyboard to highlight it and press Return. Safari will immediately take you to that existing tab instead of loading the page again.

How to Switch to an Open Tab on iPhone and iPad

The feature works identically on mobile devices running iOS and iPadOS, saving you the trouble of scrolling through the visual tab overview screen.

  1. Tap the Smart Search Field: Tap the address bar at the bottom or top of your Safari screen, depending on your layout preferences.
  2. Enter the Matching URL: Start typing the name or URL of the open webpage.
  3. Tap the Suggestion: Look at the list of results that appears above your keyboard. Find the matching URL that displays the Switch to Tab label directly beneath the website title and tap it. Safari will instantly switch your view to that previously opened tab.

Important Things to Know

  • Works Across Windows and Tab Groups: On a Mac, if the matching tab is open in a completely separate Safari window or tucked away inside a different Tab Group, clicking Switch to Tab will automatically bring that specific window or Tab Group to the forefront.
  • iCloud Tabs Integration: If you have a webpage open on another Apple device signed into the same Apple ID, Safari may also show that page in the dropdown menu under an iCloud Tabs section, though it will open a new tab on your current device rather than switching.
  • Private Browsing Limitation: The "Switch to Tab" feature isolates Private Browsing sessions. If you are typing in a standard Safari window, it will not offer to switch to a tab that is currently open in a Private Browsing window, and vice versa.
