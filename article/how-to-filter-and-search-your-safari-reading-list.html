How to Filter and Search Your Safari Reading List

This article provides a quick and simple guide on how to organize and navigate your Safari Reading List on Mac, iPhone, and iPad. You will learn the exact steps to filter your saved webpages to display only unread articles, as well as how to use the built-in search function to quickly locate specific saved items by their title or keyword.

Filtering and Searching on a Mac

To manage your Reading List in Safari on macOS, you first need to open the Reading List sidebar.

  1. Open the Safari browser.
  2. Click the Sidebar icon in the top-left corner of the window.
  3. Select Reading List (the eyeglasses icon) from the menu options.

How to Show Only Unread Articles: At the top of the Reading List sidebar, there is a toggle switch. Click Unread to filter out articles you have already finished. To see your entire list again, simply click All. If you do not see this toggle, scroll to the very top of the Reading List and pull down slightly to reveal it.

How to Search Saved Titles: Right above the All/Unread toggle at the top of the Reading List sidebar, you will find a search bar. Click into the Search field and type the words from the title or topic you are looking for. Safari will automatically filter your list in real-time to match your query.

Filtering and Searching on iPhone and iPad

Managing your Reading List on iOS and iPadOS follows a similar process through the Bookmarks menu.

  1. Open the Safari app.
  2. Tap the Bookmarks icon (the open book symbol) at the bottom of the screen on iPhone, or at the top-left on iPad.
  3. Tap the Reading List tab (the eyeglasses icon).

How to Show Only Unread Articles: Look at the bottom-left corner of the Reading List menu and tap Show Unread. The list will immediately update to hide any articles you have previously scrolled through to the end. Tap Show All in the same spot to return to your full list.

How to Search Saved Titles: Open your Reading List and swipe down slightly on the list of articles. This action reveals a hidden search bar at the top of the menu. Tap the Search Reading List field and type in the title or keyword of the saved webpage you want to find. Tap Cancel when you are done searching to return to the standard view.
