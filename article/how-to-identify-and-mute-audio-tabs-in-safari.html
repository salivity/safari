How to Identify and Mute Audio Tabs in Safari

This article provides a quick guide on how to locate and control audio playing in the Safari web browser. It explains the visual indicator that Safari displays on tabs actively producing sound and details the simple steps required to mute individual or multiple noisy tabs directly from the browser interface.

The Visual Audio Indicator

When a webpage plays audio in Safari, the browser displays a small speaker icon with sound waves emanating from it.

Depending on your current view, you will see this icon in two places:

  • On the Tab: The speaker icon appears on the right side of the specific tab that is generating the sound. This allows you to quickly scan your open tabs and find the source of the audio, even if you are currently viewing a different tab.
  • In the Smart Search Field (Address Bar): If any tab is playing audio, a blue or gray speaker icon will also appear on the right side of the main address bar at the top of the browser window.

How to Mute a Tab

Safari makes it easy to silence audio without having to hunt for the pause button on the actual webpage.

Muting a Single Tab

To immediately silence a tab playing audio:

  1. Locate the tab with the speaker icon.
  2. Click directly on the speaker icon once.

Once clicked, the icon will change to a speaker with a diagonal slash through it, indicating that the tab is now muted. To unmute the tab, simply click the icon again.

Muting from the Address Bar

If the tab currently on your screen is the one playing audio, you can simply click the speaker icon located on the right side of the Smart Search Field (address bar) at the top of the screen to mute and unmute it.

Muting Multiple Tabs at Once

If you have several tabs playing audio simultaneously, or if you want to silence everything except the tab you are currently watching, you can use the right-click menu:

  1. Right-click (or Control-click) the speaker icon in the Smart Search Field (address bar) or on an individual tab.
  2. Select one of the available options from the dropdown menu:
    • Mute This Tab: Silences only the selected tab.
    • Mute Other Tabs: Silences all background tabs while allowing the currently active tab to continue playing sound.
    • Mute All Tabs: Silences all audio across the entire Safari browser.
    • Go to Tab Playing Audio: Automatically switches your view to the background tab that is making noise.

These controls work similarly on iPadOS and iOS versions of Safari, where tapping the speaker icon on a tab will immediately mute its audio.
