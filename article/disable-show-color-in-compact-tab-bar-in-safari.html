Disable Show Color in Compact Tab Bar in Safari

Safari features a dynamic design that changes the color of the browser's top toolbar to match the background color of the webpage you are currently viewing. While some find this visually appealing, many users prefer a consistent, neutral system toolbar that eliminates visual distractions. This article provides a quick, step-by-step guide on how to disable the "Show color in compact tab bar" setting in Safari on a Mac to restore the standard browser appearance.

To turn off the adaptive toolbar color and return to a neutral system appearance, follow these steps:

  1. Open Safari: Launch the Safari browser on your Mac.
  2. Open Settings: Click the word Safari in the top-left corner of your screen on the macOS menu bar. From the dropdown menu, select Settings (labeled as Preferences in older versions of macOS). Alternatively, you can press Command + , on your keyboard.
  3. Select the Tabs Menu: At the top of the Settings window, click on the Tabs icon.
  4. Turn Off the Color Feature: Locate the setting labeled Show color in compact tab bar and click the checkbox next to it to remove the checkmark.

The change will take effect immediately. Your Safari toolbar will no longer adapt to website colors and will instead display the standard light or dark neutral color defined by your Mac's system-wide appearance settings.

If you notice that the "Show color in compact tab bar" option is grayed out, it means your Tab Layout is currently set to "Separate" instead of "Compact." In the "Separate" view, Safari generally defaults to a neutral toolbar automatically, though you can switch the layout to "Compact" in the same menu if you need to toggle the color checkbox specifically.
