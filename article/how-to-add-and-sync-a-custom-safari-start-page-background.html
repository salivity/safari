How to Add and Sync a Custom Safari Start Page Background

Personalizing the Safari Start Page with a custom background image is a simple way to make your web browser feel uniquely yours. This article provides a step-by-step guide on how to upload your own photos to the Safari Start Page on both Mac and iOS devices, as well as how to automatically sync your chosen background across all of your Apple devices using iCloud.

How to Upload a Custom Background on a Mac

To change your Safari Start Page background on a Mac, you can easily pull any image file directly from your computer.

  1. Open the Safari browser on your Mac.
  2. Open a new tab or window to view the Start Page.
  3. Click the Settings icon (represented by three sliders) in the bottom-right corner of the window.
  4. Check the box next to Background Image at the bottom of the pop-up menu.
  5. Click the plus (+) tile in the image selection row.
  6. A Finder window will open. Select your desired image from your computer and click Choose.

How to Upload a Custom Background on iPhone or iPad

You can also set a custom background using any image saved in your iPhone or iPad's Photos app.

  1. Open the Safari app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Tabs button and then tap the plus (+) sign to open a new, blank Start Page.
  3. Scroll to the very bottom of the page and tap the Edit button.
  4. Toggle on the switch next to Background Image.
  5. Tap the plus (+) tile to open your device's photo library.
  6. Tap the photo you want to use, then tap the close (X) button in the top-right corner of the menu to apply the changes.

How to Sync Your Background Across Devices

Once you have uploaded a custom background, you can sync it so the same image appears on your Mac, iPhone, and iPad. For this to work, all devices must be signed in to the same Apple ID, have Two-Factor Authentication turned on, and have Safari enabled in iCloud settings.

Enable Syncing in Safari:

  • On Mac: Open the Start Page, click the Settings icon in the bottom-right corner, and check the box for Use Start Page on All Devices at the very top of the menu.
  • On iPhone or iPad: Open the Start Page, scroll down and tap Edit, then toggle on the switch for Use Start Page on All Devices at the top of the menu.

Verify Your iCloud Settings: If your background is still not syncing, you need to ensure Safari is allowed to use iCloud.

  • On Mac: Go to System Settings (or System Preferences) > click your Apple ID name > iCloud. Under "Apps Using iCloud," make sure Safari is turned on.
  • On iPhone or iPad: Go to the Settings app > tap your Apple ID name > iCloud. Under "Apps Using iCloud," tap Show All and ensure the toggle next to Safari is green.

Once these settings are active, any changes you make to your Safari Start Page background on one device will automatically update across all of your other Apple devices within a few moments.
